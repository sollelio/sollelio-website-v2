# Sollelio Website V2 — Owner-approved decisions

**Status:** Founder decision record, consolidated 16 October 2026.  
**Owner:** Hélio Gonçalves.  
**Purpose:** Authoritative business and editorial decisions for the Sollelio website project. This document supersedes conflicting *recommendations* in earlier website strategy documents, but does not supersede canonical product implementation evidence, legal requirements, or approved brand assets.

## Working principles

- Sollelio is the company/masterbrand. Sollelio Events V2 and Sollelio Beauty OS are distinct products.
- Prioritise building and reviewing an excellent website over additional rounds of architecture audits and paperwork. Keep implementation lean and proportionate to a marketing website.
- The existing `sollelio.com` must remain untouched until explicit launch approval.
- Avoid invented functionality, testimonials, dates, numerical impact claims, scarcity and commercial promises.
- The founder approved **local website implementation** as the next practical step in the subsequent conversation; that does **not** automatically authorise committing, pushing, activating real-data forms or deploying. Explicitly request those actions at their respective checkpoint.

## Decision register: 0–29

### P0 — foundational and recruitment decisions

**0. Live screenshots — confirmed.** Current screenshots of the earlier Do Luxo à Mesa system show fictitious/demonstration data, according to the founder. Preserve the distinction between demo content and personal data. Rights of identifiable people in photographs still require care.

**1. Website direction — approved.** Sollelio as masterbrand; company homepage; distinct Events and Beauty OS product pages; dedicated Founding Design Partners page; truthful maturity/status messaging; premium light and dark modes. Astro + TypeScript + Tailwind, static-first on Netlify is the agreed technical direction, subject to practical implementation checks.

**2. Programme name — approved.** Public pt-PT name: **Programa de Parceiros Fundadores**. English: **Founding Design Partners**. Participation does not grant equity, company ownership or corporate decision-making rights.

**3. Programme terms — commercial and operational terms approved; legal details pending.** Initial cohort **3–5 businesses**, without fabricated scarcity. Initial term **3 months**, extendable by mutual agreement. Participation approximately **1–2 flexible hours per month**. Free during initial three months, with **no purchase obligation**. Sollelio intends to consider preferential terms after the programme but guarantees no particular pricing, discount, permanent free access or commercial benefit. Activities may include discovery discussions, prototype reviews, workflow feedback and tests when suitable versions exist. Partners influence priorities; Sollelio has final product decisions. Applications are open to the wider events ecosystem in Portugal, including organisers, caterers, decorators, venues, rental businesses and related providers; no minimum turnover or business size. Selection depends on product fit and potential for collaboration. Either party can leave at any time upon communicating the decision, without penalty. Process: short application, review, initial conversation if invited (**20–30 minutes**), invitation and confirmation. Initial response within **5 business days** of application receipt. Application does not guarantee admission. Confidentiality, handling of contributions, personal data and acceptance mechanics still require definition before the programme opens.

**4. Events product truth — approved.** **Events V2 is in discovery and architecture, with no functional implementation.** The previous operational system built for Do Luxo à Mesa is distinct from V2. Narrative: operational experience in a real business → vision of a new broader SaaS → collaboration with founding partners from conception. No V1 capability may be represented as available in V2. Do not promise launch dates, access or fixed feature roadmap. Initial public messaging should be simple, rather than an extensive per-feature status board.

**5. **6. ## Publishing blockers (do not block local page design)

- Legal/privacy review and required responsible-person contact details; privacy contact email and notifications inbox must be real and tested.
- Final verification of Netlify plan/forms/analytics and any required processor agreements or transfer measures.
- Approval of the exact brand variants, product visuals and third-party image rights to be used.
- Verified descriptions of Beauty OS functionality; no claim of working V2 functionality.
- Correct error handling and end-to-end testing of real submission flow before activating real data collection.
- Real Netlify preview validation: redirects, security headers, CSP, indexing, domain migration and accessibility/performance.
- Explicit founder authorisation before pushing, deploying or changing the live domain.

## Delivery directive

Prioritise a complete local, visually reviewable website: homepage, `/events`, partner programme, `/beauty-os`, confirmation/privacy pages, pt-PT and English, mobile, light/dark. Work from existing M0, existing VVarelAI/design materials and official logos. Keep forms in safe demonstration mode until launch requirements are satisfied. Avoid repeated audits or new approval requests for ordinary implementation details. Make changes locally, test pragmatically, show the visual result, and stop before any commit/push/deploy unless explicitly approved.
